# Code of Conduct

EvolveBench welcomes contributions from anyone interested in benchmarking
evolutionary and AI-driven code optimization. We expect participants in issues,
pull requests, and discussions to:

- Be respectful and constructive in feedback and disagreement.
- Assume good faith. Most disputes here are about task design or evaluator
  correctness, not people.
- Focus criticism on the work (a task, a benchmark result, a piece of code), not the
  contributor.
- Refrain from harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind.

Reports of unacceptable behavior can be sent to the maintainer at
rcsuwandi@gmail.com. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that
violate this code, and may block contributors for repeated or severe violations.

This is a short, project-specific code of conduct. For a fuller reference, see the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
